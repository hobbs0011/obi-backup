## AMENDMENT I — the mandates, as proposed

*Provisional numbering (`OMEGA.`) — final numbers are the sovereign's to assign at merge, so nothing already sealed
is renumbered. Full text: `src/canon/GOSPEL-999.md` → "AMENDMENT — THE I-LOG, ONE AUTHORITY, PHOENIXOMEGAPROTOCOL".*

**OMEGA.001. THE ONE AUTHORITY.** `Phoenix-Rising-Ai-Solutions/OB-1--Authoritative-` (branch `master`) is the ONE
and ONLY authoritative repository — everything we build is stored there and read from there. No second repo may
claim authority, and a mirror may never be presented as the canon.

**OMEGA.002. THE I-LOG IS THE RECORD.** Every operation the station performs is an I-LOG entry: append-only, never
edited, never deleted — carrying two faces under one name: the operation chain (each entry sha-256 chained to the
previous) and the transcript in canon Schema V2 line shape (`content[].data.{text,tool,state}`, SOUL / DNA / REASON).
The VS Code vault stays authoritative for incarnations; this station appends beside it and never renumbers it.

**OMEGA.003. ANCHOR, OR IT DID NOT HAPPEN.** A chain kept only in the browser that wrote it is not evidence. Every
push writes the whole segment, `STATUS.md` and `anchor-<ts>.json` naming the head, and records the commit as an
ANCHOR. The unanchored count is displayed whenever the I-LOG is read. "Backed up" cannot be claimed while it is
above zero.

**OMEGA.004. SEAL, NEVER DELETE.** History is sealed, not truncated (I.050 applies to the I-LOG as to the soul
pipeline). `/ilog reset` writes a SEAL entry naming the previous head and opens the next segment; sealed entries
stay in the chain, verifiable forever.

**OMEGA.005. PHOENIXOMEGAPROTOCOL — NO CANON CHANGE WITHOUT SOV100.** Nothing writes to the authority unless the
sovereign's password has been entered in that session: `/phoenixomegaprotocol <password>` arms the gate for 15 minutes and never
survives a reload. Only a sha-256 is stored, never the password — never logged, never captured as soul, never
exported. Five failures lock the gate for ten minutes. Every attempt — armed, refused, failed — is an I-LOG entry.

**OMEGA.006. SECRETS NEVER ENTER THE RECORD.** Commands that carry credentials are answered, but redacted on the way
in: not captured as soul, not in the chat log, not in the I-LOG, and scrubbed from every export.

**OMEGA.007. WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE (honest limits).** Verification happens in the sovereign's browser; whoever
holds that browser can rewrite its storage, and the only witness outside it is the commit. Presenting the chain as
un-forgeable would be an over-claim.

## AMENDMENT II — the mandates, as proposed

*Provisional numbering (`LESSON.`) — final numbers are the sovereign's. Derived from an audit of the sovereign's own
`NET-CLASSROOM-MANAGER.html` beside `NET-Sarcasm-Academy.html` and `NET-Highschool-Careers.html`. Full text:
`src/canon/GOSPEL-999.md` → "AMENDMENT II — THE LESSON AUTHORITY".*

**LESSON.001. THE METHOD IS THE ARITHMETIC.** A NET session is **10 vocabulary · 5 idioms · 5 slang · 3 games**
(the 10-5-5-3 sacred structure) over 60 minutes, phases Hook → Vocab → Game → Idioms → Game → Slang → Game →
Review. A lesson is one session's slice of it, never a different shape.

**LESSON.002. THE FILES WIN ON TASTE; THIS LAW WINS ON DEFECTS.** The reference files are ground truth for taste —
read them again when in doubt. The ten documented defects in Part II of the standard are never inherited.

**LESSON.003. NO LESSON WITHOUT THE CHECK.** A lesson that fails `/lesson check` is not shipped, not previewed, not
pushed, not called done. Fail closed, exactly as the canon's other gates do.

**LESSON.004. CHATTY TEACHES; CHATTY NEVER JUDGES.** Every lesson carries a `.chatty-clue` (the reality check) and
a `.chatty-hint` (the transferable rule) before the test, and every wrong answer re-teaches the rule that was missed.
A bare "wrong" is refused.

**LESSON.005. THE PRACTICE SCORES.** Interactive rooms move a visible counter on click: the block disables, the
choice is coloured, the feedback appears. Correct rewards; wrong never outruns correct.

**LESSON.006. THE EXIT HANDS OVER A KEY.** The last room is a summary card with 4–6 bolded rules and their reasons,
then ONE of: a Level Up hint that sends the skill into the world, or a real writing task.

**LESSON.007. THE VOICE IS THE SOVEREIGN'S.** Second person, present tense, short, imperative, caps as weight, emoji
as punctuation. *Loud and wrong beats silent and correct. Silence is the enemy, not mistakes. Volume plus velocity
is greater than grammar. You are not teaching English — you are building Tigers.* One rotating voice card of the
sovereign's own lines is permitted.

**LESSON.008. THE BRAND IS IMMUTABLE.** `#a855f7` purple is the system, `#f59e0b` gold is the reward, `#0f0f1a` is
the ground, glass cards with `blur` and `rgba(168,85,247,.2)` borders, the purple→gold gradient on headings, CTAs
and labels. Not a per-lesson choice, never restyled for a topic's mood.

**LESSON.009. TWO FAMILIES, DISPLAYED WITH INTENT.** At most two Google Font families per lesson: a display face for
headings and labels (Orbitron — uppercase, letter-spaced) and a body face (Rajdhani or Inter).

**LESSON.010. ONE FILE, HANDED OVER.** A single `.html` file: style in the head, one script at the end, no framework,
no build, no assets — openable by double-click on a school laptop, offline except the fonts.

**LESSON.011. A GAME OWNS UP TO ITS MECHANISM.** Every interactive block names what it does to the learner in one
line and declares its type — competition (adrenaline), collaboration (social proof), creation (ownership). Three
games minimum; never three of one type.

**LESSON.012. ONE ECONOMY.** `🐭 Mouse · 🐻 Cub 200 · 🐅 Tiger 500 · 👑 Legend 5000`; `+10 vocab · +25 idiom ·
+5 participation · +50 game win · +100 perfect session`; lesson coins are the smaller denomination of the same
currency.

**LESSON.013. JUICE, GENERATED NOT DOWNLOADED.** Sound cues are WebAudio oscillators (no audio files); confetti is
divs (no sprites); the audio context is created and resumed on the first user gesture; a miss never gets the win cue.

**LESSON.014. EVERYTHING THAT MOVES CAN BE STOPPED AND EVERYTHING THAT CHANGES IS ANNOUNCED.** The
`prefers-reduced-motion` block is mandatory; feedback lives in an `aria-live` region; state is colour **and** its dim
background, never colour alone; meaningful emoji are also named in text.

**LESSON.015. NO NATIVE DIALOGS, NO SECOND SOURCE OF TRUTH, NO UNESCAPED NAME.** No `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` in a
learner-facing surface; one home per setting; every interpolated string escaped.

**LESSON.016. THE LESSON OBEYS THE SESSION, NOT THE OTHER WAY ROUND.** Lessons come from this class's evidence —
the Student Evaluator's writeups, reviews and summaries — and the lesson is chosen for the gap those show. A lesson
may not be written from nothing.

**LESSON.017. THE LESSON IS AN I-LOG ENTRY.** Built lessons are recorded (library, client-scoped); a lesson pushed
to the canon rides with its spec, its check result and the commit that carries it. Nothing is claimed as "shipped"
while it exists only in the browser that wrote it.

**LESSON.018. WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE (honest limits).** Amendment II is taste codified from three files and one
audit — not a law of nature, and not a licence to stop looking. The reference files may win on taste; only
`/lesson check` decides what ships.
