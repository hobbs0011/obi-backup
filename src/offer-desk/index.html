1 · shoot this

Six frames, with a phone, in one walk around the place

You do not need to be a professional. Shoot it vertical (hold the phone upright), walk slowly, and do not stop recording between the shots — one long take is better than six clips. It takes about three minutes.

2 · drop it here

Your video, your pictures, your voice — read on your own device

Nothing is uploaded. Your browser opens the file where it sits, so there is no size limit and no waiting: a 4 GB video costs exactly what a 4 MB one costs. Close this tab and it is all gone — so send the bundle when the desk has it (step 4).

Drop your walkthrough video here — or tap to choose
Video: mp4 · mov · webm · whatever your phone records. Pictures: jpg · png · heic if the browser can read it. Several of each at once is fine.
3 · what the desk reads

It measures your footage, then builds you a reel

The desk watches your video properly: it walks it, measures where the life actually is (movement and sound), pulls the frames at those moments, and lays them out as a sheet you can look at. Then it renders a vertical reel — with movement, fades and your own words on it — on your device. The reel is silent by design: it is built from your frames, and the sound and the music are added later, on my side.
