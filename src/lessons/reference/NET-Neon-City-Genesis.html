📚 10 URBAN VOCABULARY

🏙️
URBANISATION
Đô thị hóa
The process of making an area more urban
"Rapid urbanisation changed my hometown completely."
🏢
HIGH-RISE
Nhà cao tầng
Tall buildings with many floors
"High-rise apartments dominate the skyline now."
🏘️
LOW-RISE
Nhà thấp tầng
Buildings with few stories
"My grandparents still live in a low-rise house."
🏪
CONVENIENCE STORE
Cửa hàng tiện lợi
Small shop open late hours
"I buy snacks at the convenience store every night."
🐟
WET MARKET
Chợ tươi sống
Traditional market selling fresh meat/fish
"My mom prefers shopping at the wet market."
🛣️
INFRASTRUCTURE
Cơ sở hạ tầng
Roads, power, and systems of a city
"Better infrastructure attracts more investment."
🚗
CONGESTION
Sự tắc nghẽn
Too much traffic or people
"Traffic congestion is worse during rush hour."
🏭
INDUSTRIALISATION
Công nghiệp hóa
Developing industries on a wide scale
"Industrialisation created many jobs in our province."
🚶
MIGRATION
Sự di cư
Moving from one place to another (Rural to Urban)
"Rural-to-urban migration increased last decade."
♻️
SUSTAINABLE
Bền vững
Able to be maintained at a certain rate or level
"We need sustainable development for our future."

💬 5 CITY LIFE IDIOMS

CONCRETE JUNGLE
"A city with too many ugly buildings"
Ex: "HCMC feels like a concrete jungle sometimes."
Thành phố bê tông
HUSTLE AND BUSTLE
"Busy, noisy activity of a city"
Ex: "I love the hustle and bustle of city life."
Lối sống hối hả
GHOST TOWN
"A place with no people"
Ex: "During Tết, the city becomes a ghost town."
Thành phố ma
PAVE THE WAY
"Create conditions for development"
Ex: "New roads pave the way for businesses."
Mở đường
HIT THE TOWN
"To go out and have fun in the city"
Ex: "Let's hit the town this Saturday night!"
Đi quẩy
