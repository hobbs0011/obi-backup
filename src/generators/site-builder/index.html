◇ The brief
The shape library is the wing's own shipped sites (examples/, and the venue shape from a client site that went live): a build follows that grammar's structure and restraint but writes fresh HTML for your subject. /design venue does the same from the command face.
A client's page must not leave the builder wearing our tokens — the finished file is measured against exactly that, and the log names any leak. Photos stay hosted where they are: the engine gets the URLs and is forbidden from inventing one. The model cannot see a photo, so a description after | becomes that image's alt text verbatim; an undescribed photo gets alt="" rather than a guess.
The model caps its reply, so a page is written in parts (an unfinished part ends with <<<MORE>>>) and stitched. Nothing is written until the brief is captured verbatim as Soul DNA.
