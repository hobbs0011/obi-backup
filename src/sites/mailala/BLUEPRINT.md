## 7 · THE DARK TURN (2026-09-23 — the sovereign, verbatim)

> "I hate the coloring, go dark mode"
> "dark highlights the bright photos"

**What it overrides, honestly:** SOUL-06 ("too dark of a background") was the ruling on the *previous* site's dark theme. The sovereign has now reversed it for this build, and gave his reason: the venue's own photographs are bright — neon, fairy lights, plants — so a near-black ground makes them the only lit thing on the page.

**What changed:** ground `#0e110f` (warm near-black, faint 34px gold blueprint grid), panels `#151a16`, ink `#f3ede1`, accents brightened for dark (forest `#3fa06a`, gold `#e2b45a`, crimson `#d24a33`). Every white surface — dish cards, room cards, quote cards, rule cards, FAQ panels, the contact card, the phone bar — was re-skinned; the footer and the hero shade went near-black; the room pills' unselected state is now dark, not white.

**What did NOT change:** every word, every price, every photograph, every refusal. Dark mode is a palette, not a rewrite.

**Also found and fixed while doing it:** the entrance animation was gated on an IntersectionObserver, and in a throttled preview **51 blocks stayed at opacity 0** — and a crawler would never have seen them either. The gate is gone; content is visible without JS, and the page's only motion is CSS (Ken Burns, the marquee, the image shimmer).

**Verified after the change** (rendered in the live preview, computed styles read back): body `rgb(14,17,15)`, panel `rgb(21,26,22)`, ink `rgb(243,237,225)`, 0 reveal blocks hidden, 11/11 photographs decoding, 0 broken, story title and its gold rule measured 0 px apart (no overlap), pills `#1b211c` unselected and green when on.
