What can you actually do in English?

Not what you studied — what you can do. This is a real adaptive test, not a quiz: the items move with your answers, the listening section is spoken aloud, and the result comes with every item, the reason for it, and a plan for the next four weeks.

How it measuresstated, not hidden
A staircase, not a quizCorrect moves you up a CEFR band; wrong moves you down. Two decisions in the same direction are needed before the band changes, so one guess cannot swing your level.
Placed by band, never by luckYour level is the highest band answered at 60% or better with at least three items in it. Below three items a band is listed UNPLACED rather than guessed.
The limit is namedListening is spoken by your device's own voice — a real listening task, but not a recorded examiner. The result card says which voice was used.

After the test

What you get, and what it is worth.

01

A CEFR level with its working

A1 to C2, plus the per-band table showing exactly which bands were answered, how many items in each, and where the placement came from.

02

A skill breakdown

Grammar, vocabulary, usage, reading and listening, separately scored — because a student is rarely uniformly bad, and the weak skill is the one to fix first.

03

A 45/15 plan for four weeks

Concrete input and output prescriptions for your band, and a target you can measure at the end of the month.

What the test is not. It is not the coach's diagnostic and it does not replace it. The free 30-minute diagnostic is a conversation, in person or online, where the mode (Mouse · Cub · Tiger) is read from behaviour — something no browser can see. Use this to arrive at that conversation with a number.
