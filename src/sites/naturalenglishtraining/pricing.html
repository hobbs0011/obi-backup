      Why is it per hour and not a package?

      Because packages are how a centre hides a discount it can withdraw and a commitment a student cannot keep. Hours are honest: you buy what you use, and if the plan changes — a school term ends, a job posts you to Hanoi — nothing is stranded in a package.

      Do you offer a block discount?

      Sometimes, and it is the coach's decision, quoted after the diagnostic. The calculator on this page shows a group reduction when more than one learner shares the hour, marked clearly as pending his confirmation — it is an indication, not a price.

      What if my child hates it after two weeks?

      Tell the coach. Sessions are 1-on-1 and the material is chosen for the student, so “hates it” is usually a signal that the material is wrong, not that the student is. If it genuinely is not working, stop — there is no package to strand and no cancellation penalty beyond the notice in your written terms.

      Is there a cheaper online rate?

      No. The hour is the hour — same preparation, same plan, same 1-on-1 attention; only the room differs. Charging less for a video call would be charging less for the same work, and it would make the in-home families subsidise it.

      How do I pay?

      Directly to the coach, by the method you already use — bank transfer or cash — and confirmed in writing before the first paid hour. There is no card form on this website, because this website never takes your money.
