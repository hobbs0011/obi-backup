Part one

Privacy — in one paragraph, then in detail.

This website has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies of its own, and no mailing list. Anything you type is stored in your own browser on your own device. It is sent to NET only if you press a button that sends it — WhatsApp, email, SMS, the share sheet, or a file download. Until then, it has not left your phone.

In detail: what is stored, and where

What is never done with it

  • Never sold, rented, or shared with a marketing list.
  • Never used to train an AI model.
  • Never used to send you anything you did not ask for. There is no newsletter to unsubscribe from, because there is no newsletter.
  • Never used for a child's data beyond arranging their lessons. A child's name and age go to the coach and to nobody else.

If the API lane is switched on later

When the coach deploys the site's own back end (the code ships with the site under backend/), submissions chosen for sending will be stored on a server he controls, so that an enquiry made on a tablet at a café is not lost when the browser is closed. This page will be updated before that happens, and the booking form will show the API lane's real state at the moment you submit — as it already does.

Your rights, practically

Because the data is on your device, the strongest right is the simplest: clear it. The booking page has a button that reads back and downloads everything stored, and the “Show what is stored” panel lists it in full. If you have already sent an enquiry to the coach and want it deleted, message him and it will be deleted — no retention policy will be cited at you, because there isn't one worth citing.

Part two

Terms — the free diagnostic, and the guarantee.

The free 30-minute diagnostic

Thirty minutes, free, in your home within the home zone (Lakeview City, District 2 and surrounding districts) or online elsewhere. It is not conditional on buying anything, there is no deposit, and there is no automatic follow-up you cannot decline. It includes a real assessment of level and a plan; it does not include teaching beyond one immediate win.

Paid tuition

  • Rate. 600,000 VND per hour for one-to-one tuition, in-home, as published on the pricing page. Blocks, groups and corporate work are quoted in writing before they start.
  • Payment. Directly to the coach by the method agreed with you. This website never processes a payment and never asks for card details.
  • Scheduling and cancellations. A session moved with at least 12 hours' notice is not charged. Shorter notice may be charged. The exact rule is confirmed in writing before your first paid hour, so it is never a surprise.
  • Materials. Included. There is no registration fee, no materials fee and no platform subscription.

The 100% Confidence Guarantee, as written

We do not promise perfect grammar in 30 days. We promise that a student who follows the 45/15 protocol will completely lose the fear of opening their mouth to speak English.

Take the first month. If there is no massive, measurable surge in speaking velocity and confidence, the tuition is refunded in full. The student risks nothing.

Quoted as written from NET's own doctrine (GOSPEL 444 · G444.011). How it is judged: against a recording of the same speaking task taken on day one and on day thirty, and against the tracked 45/15 ratio. It is conditional on the protocol actually being followed; a student who does not do the 45/15 is not entitled to the refund, and the coach will say so plainly rather than let a month pass in silence.

What is not promised

  • Not a specific exam band or school grade within a fixed time. Exam outcomes depend on the student's teacher, syllabus, exam date and effort.
  • Not a CEFR jump on a fixed schedule. The free placement test's own limits are stated on its result card.
  • Not availability at every hour requested. One coach teaches a limited number of students, and the honest answer to “can you take four more?” is sometimes no.
