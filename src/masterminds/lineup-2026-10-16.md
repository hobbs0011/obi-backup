# Lineup doctrine — wings vs masterminds (2026-10-16, sovereign-approved direction)

Sovereign's position: set up as many wings as possible without overdoing it. If there is demand — or internal need — lay the foundation early. Do not carry all eggs in one basket: NET monetizes as the forward face; the rest are options that pay the moment demand appears.

## The rule

- A WING (division, scaffold, recipe) costs ~zero attention. Proliferate freely on demand-signal or internal need.
- A MASTERMIND (separate station under command) costs the sovereign's leadership. A wing graduates only with: (1) a distinct audience, (2) real demand — paying users or daily internal use, (3) an operator leading it day-to-day.
- Every piece carries a MODE (MONETIZE or FOUNDATION) and a one-line DEMAND THESIS (who pays/uses, what proves it). No thesis, no wing.
- Split by mission, never by layer. Forge and Sentinel are family-wide functions (hands and shield), not product divisions.
