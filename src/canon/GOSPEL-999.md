### CANON.002 — NO FALSE SCRIPT STANDS IN FOR A CANON ONE

No script, module, command, registry row, card or doctrine line may present itself as canon unless it **names the
canon file it derives from**. Where no canon source exists, the thing is one of exactly two:

1. labelled **STATION** — invented here, and said so on its own face; or
2. **withdrawn from service**.

There is no third state. A thing that is neither sourced nor labelled is a false script, and a false script on a
canon-facing surface is a breach, not a cosmetic defect.

The `.kilo/command/` registry (46 files, branch `master`) is the **legacy Kilo lineage**. It is not authority. It

A false script is **withdrawn from service and the withdrawal is logged in the I-LOG**. `SEAL, NEVER DELETE`
governs the **record**: a sealed record is never rewritten, and a correction is appended beside it. It does not
keep a false script in service. Deleting the *claim* is not deleting the *history of the claim* — the history is
the I-LOG entry that says it was withdrawn, by whom, and why.
